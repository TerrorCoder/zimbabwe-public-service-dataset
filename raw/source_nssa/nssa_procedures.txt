NATIONAL SOCIAL SECURITY AUTHORITY (NSSA) - ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES

1. EMPLOYER SOCIAL SECURITY REGISTRATION (PENSION & ACCIDENT PREVENTION SCHEMES) (SVC-016)
- Category: social_security
- Requirements: Certificate of Incorporation / PBC Certificate, Form CR6 / CR5, Director IDs, List of Employees with IDs, Company Bank Details, Form P2 (Employer Registration).
- Official Fee: USD $0 (Statutory registration; monthly contributions payable thereafter).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Complete NSSA Employer Registration Form P2 online (selfservice.nssa.org.zw) or at NSSA Regional Office.
  2. Attach company incorporation documents, director IDs, and initial payroll list.
  3. NSSA inspector assigns Employer SSR Number.
  4. Access NSSA Self-Service Portal to upload monthly Form P4 returns.
- Processing Time: 2 - 3 working days.

2. EMPLOYEE PENSION & INVALIDITY GRANT REGISTRATION (SVC-017)
- Category: social_security
- Requirements: Employee National ID Copy, Birth Certificate, NSSA Social Security Number (SSN), Employer Form P3 (Employee Registration), Certified Bank Account Details.
- Official Fee: USD $0.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Employer registers employee via Form P3 within 14 days of employment start.
  2. Employee issued unique NSSA Social Security Number.
  3. Monthly 4.5% employee + 4.5% employer contribution remitted to NSSA.
- Processing Time: Immediate upon employer submission.

3. VOLUNTARY INFORMAL SECTOR PENSION SCHEME (VIPS) (SVC-018)
- Category: social_security
- Requirements: National Identity Card, Proof of Informal Business Activity / Vendor License, Passport photo, Nominated beneficiary details.
- Official Fee: USD $0 registration; minimum $10/month voluntary contribution.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Self-employed or informal trader visits NSSA branch or mobile registration kiosk.
  2. Fill in VIPS Registration Form.
  3. Select monthly contribution tier (USD or ZiG equivalent).
  4. Receive NSSA VIPS Membership Card and mobile payment shortcode.
- Processing Time: Same day.
