IMMIGRATION DEPARTMENT OF ZIMBABWE & LOCAL CITY COUNCIL PROCEDURES

1. TEMPORARY EMPLOYMENT PERMIT (TEP / WORK PERMIT) (SVC-022)
- Agency: Immigration Department of Zimbabwe
- Category: civil_registration
- Requirements: Form IF1, Employer Letter of Justification (proving no local citizen possesses specialized skill), Curriculum Vitae, Certified Qualifications, Police Clearance Certificate from country of origin, Medical Clearance, Application Fee.
- Official Fee: USD $500 Application Fee + USD $1,500 Annual Permit Fee upon approval.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Employer submits TEP application form IF1 to Department of Immigration Headquarters (Linquenda House, Harare).
  2. Sub-Committee on Foreign Employment reviews specialized skill justification and local understudy training plan.
  3. Security vetting by relevant security agencies.
  4. Board decision and approval letter issued.
  5. Payment of annual permit fee and endorsement of TEP sticker in passport.
- Processing Time: 30 - 60 calendar days.

2. BUSINESS INVESTOR RESIDENCE PERMIT (SVC-023)
- Agency: Immigration Department of Zimbabwe
- Category: civil_registration
- Requirements: Zimbabwe Investment and Development Agency (ZIDA) License, Proof of Capital Investment (minimum $100,000 USD banked into Zimbabwe), Business Incorporation Docs, Bank Statements, Police Clearance, Medical Certificate.
- Official Fee: USD $500 Application Fee + USD $2,000 Investor Permit Fee.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Obtain ZIDA Investor License.
  2. Submit Residence Permit application form to Immigration Headquarters.
  3. Financial audit verifying capital inflow and employment creation for Zimbabweans.
  4. Issuance of 5-Year Renewable Investor Residence Permit.
- Processing Time: 30 - 45 calendar days.

3. MUNICIPAL BUSINESS OPERATING LICENCE (SHOP LICENCE) (SVC-024)
- Agency: City Council (Harare / Bulawayo / Local Authorities)
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Company Incorporation / PBC Docs, ZIMRA Tax Clearance (ITF263), Approved Building Floor Plan, Fire Clearance Certificate, City Health Inspection Certificate, Advertisement Notice in local newspaper.
- Official Fee: USD $150 - $450 per annum depending on business activity zone and floor area.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Collect Shop Licence application form from Town House / Municipal District Office.
  2. Publish public notice of intention to operate in two consecutive editions of local newspaper (14-day objection window).
  3. Undergo joint inspection by City Health, Fire Department, and Town Planning officers.
  4. Submit inspection clearance reports and pay annual licence fee.
  5. City Licensing Officer issues Municipal Business Licence disc for display.
- Processing Time: 14 - 30 calendar days.

4. MUNICIPAL HEALTH & FOOD HANDLER'S MEDICAL CERTIFICATE (SVC-025)
- Agency: City Council Health Department
- Category: civil_registration
- Requirements: National ID Copy, 2 Passport photos, Stool/Blood Medical Test Results from designated municipal clinic, Medical examination fee.
- Official Fee: USD $15 per worker per annum.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Food handling staff report to Municipal Clinic (e.g. Beatrice Road Infectious Diseases Hospital / City Clinic).
  2. Complete medical examination and lab testing for communicable diseases.
  3. Doctor signs off health clearance.
  4. City Health Department issues Food Handler's Medical Card (Red Card).
- Processing Time: 2 - 3 working days.
