MINISTRY OF MINES, AGRICULTURE & TIMB ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES

1. PROSPECTING LICENCE & MINING CLAIM REGISTRATION (SVC-033)
- Agency: Ministry of Mines and Mining Development
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Applicant National ID / Company Registration Certificate, Certificate of Financial Capability, Form M1 Prospecting Application, Mining District Locator Map, Landowner Notification Acknowledgment.
- Official Fee: USD $200 per prospecting licence (valid for 2 years) + USD $100 claim pegging fee.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Submit Form M1 at Provincial Mining Commissioner's Office.
  2. Purchase Prospecting Licence token ($200).
  3. Engage registered Approved Prospector to peg mining claim.
  4. Post Prospecting Notice (DPN) on ground for 30 days.
  5. File Registration Certificate Application with GPS coordinates and sketch plan.
  6. Mining Commissioner issues Mining Claim Certificate of Registration.
- Processing Time: 30 - 45 calendar days.

2. TOBACCO GROWER REGISTRATION & MARKETING NUMBER (SVC-036)
- Agency: Tobacco Industry and Marketing Board (TIMB)
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Grower National ID, Proof of Land Tenure / Title Deed / Offer Letter / A2 Permit / Communal Chief's Letter, Farm GPS Location, Bank Account Details.
- Official Fee: USD $10 (Early registration before October 31); USD $40 (Late registration).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Access TIMB Portal (portal.timb.co.zw) or visit TIMB Regional Office.
  2. Complete Grower Registration Form detailing farm hectarage and tobacco variety.
  3. Upload proof of land occupation and ID.
  4. Pay registration fee ($10 early bird).
  5. Receive TIMB Grower Sales Code Number for auction floor delivery.
- Processing Time: 1 - 2 working days.

3. LIVESTOCK MOVEMENT PERMIT & VETERINARY CLEARANCE (SVC-035)
- Agency: Department of Veterinary Services (DVS)
- Category: civil_registration
- Requirements: Stock Movement Application Form, Police Clearance (Clearance Form 392 for cattle), Veterinary Inspection Certificate (proving Foot and Mouth / Anthrax vaccination status), Buyer & Seller IDs.
- Official Fee: USD $5 per movement permit + USD $1 per head of cattle.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Obtain Police Stock Theft clearance (Form 392) at local ZRP station.
  2. Request Veterinary Officer to inspect cattle/livestock at dip tank or farm.
  3. Vet officer verifies brand marks, ear tags, and health status.
  4. Pay veterinary movement fee.
  5. Receive DVS Livestock Movement Permit valid for 7 days.
- Processing Time: 1 - 2 working days.
