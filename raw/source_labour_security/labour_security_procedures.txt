LABOUR, ENERGY, ENVIRONMENT & SECURITY PROCEDURES

1. FIREARM / GUN LICENCE APPLICATION & RENEWAL (SVC-145)
- Agency: Zimbabwe Republic Police (Controller of Firearms) / Ministry of Home Affairs
- Category: civil_registration
- Requirements: Applicant National ID, Passed Safe Storage Audit (Gun Safe bolted to wall/floor), CID Criminal Vetting Clearance, Reason for Possession (Farming, Game protection, Cash in Transit, Self-defence), Form FR 1.
- Official Fee: USD $50 Application Fee + USD $20 Annual Renewal Fee.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Obtain Form FR 1 from local ZRP station firearms registry.
  2. Local ZRP Officer-in-Charge conducts physical site inspection of gun safe storage.
  3. Fingerprints vetted by CID Criminal Records Office.
  4. File forwarded to Controller of Firearms (Police General Headquarters).
  5. Controller issues Firearm Certificate valid for 3 years.
- Processing Time: 30 - 60 calendar days.

2. LIQUOR LICENSING BOARD OPERATING PERMIT (SVC-149)
- Agency: Liquor Licensing Board (Ministry of Local Government / Home Affairs)
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Approved Municipal Health Sanitation Certificate, Chief Fire Officer Clearance, Police Vetting Certificate, Press Notice in 2 Newspapers, Form LLB 1.
- Official Fee: USD $100 Application Fee + USD $300 - $800 Annual Licence (Bar/Bottle Store/Nightclub).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Publish notice of application in 2 editions of national newspaper for 14-day public review.
  2. Obtain Health Inspection clearance from Municipal Health Officer and Fire Department.
  3. Submit Form LLB 1 to Secretary of the Liquor Licensing Board.
  4. Board conducts quarterly licensing hearing.
  5. Receive official Liquor Operating Licence for display at premises.
- Processing Time: 30 - 60 calendar days.

3. CASINO & GAMING HOUSE OPERATING LICENCE (SVC-150)
- Agency: Lotteries and Gaming Board
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Company Incorporation (Pvt Ltd), Paid-up Capital ($500,000 USD), Security Surveillance System (CCTV), Fit and Proper Vetting of Shareholders & Directors, Form LGB 1.
- Official Fee: USD $2,500 Application Fee + USD $10,000 Annual Operating Licence.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Submit formal application Form LGB 1 to Lotteries and Gaming Board Secretariat.
  2. Background security vetting conducted by ZRP CID.
  3. Technical inspectors audit gaming machines, roulette tables, and CCTV coverage.
  4. Board approves licence and issues Gaming House Licence.
- Processing Time: 60 - 90 calendar days.
