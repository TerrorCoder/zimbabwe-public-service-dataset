SOLAR ENERGY, GMB & EDUCATION PROCEDURES

1. INDEPENDENT POWER PRODUCER (IPP) SOLAR/HYDRO LICENCE (SVC-091)
- Agency: Zimbabwe Energy Regulatory Authority (ZERA)
- Category: postal_licensing
- Requirements: Approved EMA EIA Clearance, Grid Interconnection Agreement with ZETDC, Land Lease / Title Deed, Feasibility & Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) Draft.
- Official Fee: USD $1,000 Application Fee + USD $5,000 Licence Fee (Projects > 100kW generation capacity).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Submit IPP Licence application to ZERA Licensing Department.
  2. Publish public notice in national newspapers for 14-day objection window.
  3. ZERA Engineering Committee evaluates technical feasibility and PPA structure.
  4. ZERA Board grants approval and issues IPP Generation Licence.
- Processing Time: 60 - 90 calendar days.

2. GRAIN MARKETING BOARD (GMB) FARMER REGISTRATION (SVC-077)
- Agency: Grain Marketing Board (GMB)
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Farmer National ID, Proof of Farming Land (Offer letter, title deed, or Chief's letter), Bank Account Details, Agritex Officer Verification.
- Official Fee: USD $0 (Free farmer registration).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Visit local GMB Depot or Agritex Ward Extension Officer.
  2. Present National ID and land details for verification.
  3. GMB clerk registers farmer and issues GMB Delivery Voucher Card.
  4. Deliver maize, wheat, or small grains to GMB depot for payment.
- Processing Time: Same day (30 minutes).

3. ZIMSEC EXAMINATION CERTIFICATE REPLACEMENT (SVC-089)
- Agency: Zimbabwe School Examinations Council (ZIMSEC)
- Category: civil_registration
- Requirements: Original National ID / Passport, Candidate Centre & Index Number, Year of Sitting, Police Report / Sworn Affidavit of Loss, Application Form ZIMSEC 1.
- Official Fee: USD $20 per Certificate replacement / Statement of Results.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Report lost certificate to ZRP for Police Affidavit.
  2. Submit Form ZIMSEC 1 at ZIMSEC Central Offices (Mt Pleasant, Harare or Bulawayo Regional Office).
  3. Pay $20 fee at cash counter.
  4. ZIMSEC verifies examination records and prints official Statement of Results / Replacement Certificate.
- Processing Time: 5 - 10 working days.
