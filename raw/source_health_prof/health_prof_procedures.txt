HEALTHCARE, LEGAL & PROFESSIONAL REGISTRATION PROCEDURES

1. MEDICAL PRACTITIONER ANNUAL PRACTISING CERTIFICATE (SVC-046)
- Agency: Medical and Dental Practitioners Council of Zimbabwe (MDPCZ)
- Category: civil_registration
- Requirements: MBChB / Medical Degree Certificate, Internship Completion Certificate, Certificate of Good Standing from previous medical council, CPD Credit points proof, Application Form MDPCZ 1.
- Official Fee: USD $150 per annum for general practitioners; USD $300 for specialists.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Submit Form MDPCZ 1 to Council Registrar.
  2. Attach medical degree, internship completion letter, and CPD points evidence.
  3. MDPCZ Credentials Committee audits qualifications and internship logbook.
  4. Pay annual practising fee.
  5. Receive Medical Practising Certificate and entry into the Zimbabwe Medical Register.
- Processing Time: 7 - 14 working days.

2. ESTATE AGENT REGISTRATION & PRACTISING CERTIFICATE (SVC-057 -> SVC-049)
- Agency: Estate Agents Council of Zimbabwe (EACZ)
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Estate Agency Diploma / Real Estate Degree, Passed EACZ Professional Board Exam, Fidelity Guarantee Fund Bond Certificate, Company Registration Docs, Form EAC 1.
- Official Fee: USD $200 Registration Fee + USD $300 Annual Practising Certificate.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Sit for EACZ Board Examination.
  2. Deposit Fidelity Guarantee Fund security bond.
  3. Submit application Form EAC 1 with qualification certificates to EACZ Secretariat.
  4. Council audits trust account compliance and criminal clearance.
  5. Receive Registered Estate Agent Certificate and Compensation Fund Card.
- Processing Time: 14 - 30 calendar days.

3. PRIVATE VOLUNTARY ORGANIZATION (PVO / NGO) REGISTRATION (SVC-056)
- Agency: PVO Board / Ministry of Public Service, Labour and Social Welfare
- Category: civil_registration
- Requirements: Proposed Constitution, List of Board of Trustees with IDs, Police Clearance for Trustees, District Development Coordinator (DDC) Recommendation Letter, Form PVO 1.
- Official Fee: USD $20 Application Fee + USD $100 Registration Certificate Fee.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Draft NGO Constitution detailing non-profit objectives.
  2. Publish notice of intention to register in 2 local newspapers (21-day objection period).
  3. Submit Form PVO 1 with newspaper cuttings and trustee police clearances to DDC / Provincial Office.
  4. PVO Board reviews application and security clearances.
  5. Minister signs off and issues PVO Registration Certificate.
- Processing Time: 60 - 90 calendar days.
