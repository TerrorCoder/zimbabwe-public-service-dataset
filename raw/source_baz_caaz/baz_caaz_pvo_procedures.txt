BAZ, CAAZ & ZIMPARKS ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES

1. DRONE OPERATOR LICENCE & REMOTELY PILOTED AIRCRAFT SYSTEM (RPAS) PERMIT (SVC-058)
- Agency: Civil Aviation Authority of Zimbabwe (CAAZ)
- Category: postal_licensing
- Requirements: RPAS Pilot Licence / Remote Pilot Certificate, Drone Serial Number & Import Clearance, Third Party Aviation Insurance, Airspace Security Approval (Ministry of Defence), CAAZ Application Form.
- Official Fee: USD $100 Registration Fee + USD $200 Annual Permit Fee (Commercial Drone).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Register drone hardware serial number with CAAZ Flight Safety Division.
  2. Complete Remote Pilot License training at CAAZ-certified ATO.
  3. Submit flight operations safety manual and security clearance application.
  4. CAAZ inspects drone and verifies insurance cover.
  5. Receive CAAZ RPAS Operator Certificate (ROC).
- Processing Time: 14 - 30 calendar days.

2. COMMERCIAL FISHING & KAPENTA OPERATING LICENCE (SVC-060)
- Agency: Zimbabwe Parks and Wildlife Management Authority (Zimparks)
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Vessel Certificate of Seaworthiness (Ministry of Transport / Lake Captain), Rig / Boat Serial Registration, ZIMRA Tax Clearance, Zimparks Form K1.
- Official Fee: USD $1,000 per Kapenta Rig per annum (Lake Kariba Basin).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Present Kapenta rig for marine safety inspection by Lake Captain.
  2. Submit Form K1 to Zimparks Kariba / Harare Office with seaworthiness certificate.
  3. Pay annual Kapenta quota licensing fee.
  4. Receive Zimparks Commercial Fishing Licence disc and permit token.
- Processing Time: 7 - 14 working days.
