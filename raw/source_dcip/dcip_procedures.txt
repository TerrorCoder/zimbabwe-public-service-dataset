DEEDS AND COMPANIES OFFICE (DCIP / CORES) - ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES

1. PRIVATE BUSINESS CORPORATION (PBC) REGISTRATION (SVC-005)
- Category: Business Registration
- Requirements: Proposed PBC Name (up to 5 choices), CR1 Name Availability Search Confirmation, Details of Members (1 to 20 members), National ID copies, Physical Business Address, Accounting Officer details.
- Official Fee: USD $20 (Name Search $5 + Registration Fee $15).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Submit Name Search application (Form CR1) online via CORES portal (cores.dcip.gov.zw) or at Deeds Office counter.
  2. Await Name Reservation clearance (valid for 30 days).
  3. Draft Incorporation Statement (Form PBC 2) detailing objectives, member contributions, and accounting officer.
  4. Upload signed PBC 2 form and member ID copies.
  5. Pay registration fee and receive PBC Incorporation Certificate.
- Processing Time: 3 - 5 working days.

2. PRIVATE LIMITED COMPANY (PVT LTD) INCORPORATION (SVC-006)
- Category: Business Registration
- Requirements: Approved Name Search (CR1), Memorandum & Articles of Association, Notice of Situation of Physical Office (Form CR5), Particulars of Directors & Secretaries (Form CR6), Copies of IDs of minimum 2 Directors.
- Official Fee: USD $85 (Name Search $5 + Registration $80).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Complete online name search and obtain approval.
  2. Prepare Memorandum and Articles of Association.
  3. Complete Form CR5 (Registered Address) and Form CR6 (Directors and Secretary).
  4. Submit application documents via CORES e-services.
  5. Payment of statutory registration fee.
  6. Registrar issues Certificate of Incorporation and stamped CR5/CR6 forms.
- Processing Time: 5 - 7 working days.

3. TRADEMARK REGISTRATION (SVC-007)
- Category: Intellectual Property
- Requirements: Representation of the Mark/Logo, Applicant Details, List of Goods/Services (Nice Classification), Power of Attorney (if filed via agent).
- Official Fee: USD $200 (Application Fee $100 + Publication/Registration $100).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Conduct preliminary trademark availability search at IP Office.
  2. File Application Form TM 1 with mark representation and classification.
  3. Formality examination and substantive examination by Registrar.
  4. Acceptance and publication in Patent & Trademark Journal (2 months opposition period).
  5. Grant and issuance of Trademark Registration Certificate.
- Processing Time: 6 - 12 months.
