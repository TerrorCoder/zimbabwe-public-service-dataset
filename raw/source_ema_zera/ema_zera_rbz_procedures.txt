ENVIRONMENTAL MANAGEMENT AGENCY (EMA), ZERA & RBZ PROCEDURES

1. ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT ASSESSMENT (EIA) CERTIFICATE (SVC-037)
- Agency: Environmental Management Agency (EMA)
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Project Prospectus Submission, EIA Study Report by Registered Environmental Consultant, Public Stakeholder Consultation Records, Site Layout Plans, Review Fee Payment.
- Official Fee: 1.5% of Total Project Capital Investment Cost (Minimum $210 USD).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Submit Project Prospectus to EMA Director General for screening.
  2. Engage registered independent Environmental Consultant to conduct EIA study.
  3. Conduct public stakeholder consultations and publish notice in national press.
  4. Submit comprehensive EIA Report to EMA for technical review.
  5. EMA Technical Committee inspects site and evaluates mitigation plan.
  6. Issuance of 2-Year Renewable EIA Certificate.
- Processing Time: 30 - 60 calendar days.

2. PETROLEUM RETAIL & FUEL STATION OPERATING LICENCE (SVC-042)
- Agency: Zimbabwe Energy Regulatory Authority (ZERA)
- Category: postal_licensing
- Requirements: Approved EIA Certificate from EMA, Local Authority Building & Town Planning Permit, Fire Brigade Inspection Clearance Certificate, Tank Calibration Certificates, Company Incorporation Documents, ZIMRA Tax Clearance.
- Official Fee: USD $2,000 per annum (Urban Fuel Station); USD $500 (Rural Fuel Station).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Apply for ZERA Retail License via ZERA portal (zera.co.zw).
  2. Upload EMA EIA Certificate, Fire Clearance, and local council permits.
  3. ZERA Technical Engineers conduct physical site safety inspection.
  4. Board approval and payment of annual license fee.
  5. Receive ZERA Petroleum Retailer Operating Licence.
- Processing Time: 30 - 45 calendar days.

3. BUREAU DE CHANGE OPERATING LICENCE (SVC-039)
- Agency: Reserve Bank of Zimbabwe (RBZ) / Exchange Control
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Minimum Paid-up Capital Proof ($50,000 USD banked at RBZ), Incorporation as Pvt Ltd, Anti-Money Laundering (AML/CFT) Compliance Manual, Director Vetting by CID, Secure Premises Layout.
- Official Fee: USD $1,000 Application Fee + USD $2,500 License Fee.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Submit formal application to RBZ Exchange Control Division.
  2. Deposit required minimum capital reserve ($50,000) with commercial bank.
  3. Security and AML compliance audit by Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU).
  4. RBZ Governor approves licence.
  5. Receive Bureau de Change Operating Licence disc.
- Processing Time: 60 - 90 calendar days.
