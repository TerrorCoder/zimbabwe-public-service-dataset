ZIMBABWE REVENUE AUTHORITY (ZIMRA) - ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES

1. INDIVIDUAL TAXPAYER IDENTIFICATION NUMBER (TIN) REGISTRATION (SVC-008)
- Category: Tax
- Requirements: Copy of National ID Card or Valid Passport, Proof of Residence (Utility bill or bank statement under 3 months old), Proof of Income/Employment (Paystub or bank statement).
- Official Fee: USD $0 (Free official government service).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Access TaRMS Self Service Portal (mytaxselfservice.zimra.co.zw).
  2. Click 'Sign Up' and register an SSP user account with verified mobile number and email.
  3. Select 'Register as Individual'.
  4. Fill in Personal Details, Physical Address, and Banking Details.
  5. Upload National ID and Proof of Residence.
  6. Submit application; system automatically generates Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN).
- Processing Time: Immediate online / 24 hours.

2. BUSINESS TAXPAYER ONBOARDING (BP NUMBER / TARMS) (SVC-009)
- Category: Tax
- Requirements: Certificate of Incorporation, Form CR6 (Directors), Form CR5 (Business Address), Memorandum & Articles of Association, Directors' National IDs, Stamped Company Bank Statement, Public Officer Appointment Letter.
- Official Fee: USD $0 (Free official service).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Create Organization Profile on TaRMS SSP portal.
  2. Complete Business Entity Registration wizard.
  3. Attach stamped bank statement and company incorporation documents.
  4. Designate Public Officer and attach signed appointment letter + ID.
  5. ZIMRA officer conducts backend verification and approves profile.
  6. TIN issued and Single Account linked.
- Processing Time: 1 - 3 working days.

3. VALUE ADDED TAX (VAT) REGISTRATION (SVC-010)
- Category: Tax
- Requirements: Active ZIMRA TIN, Proof of meeting threshold ($25,000 USD annual turnover or historical invoices), Physical Business premises inspection consent, Certified Bank Statement.
- Official Fee: USD $0 (Free official service).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Submit VAT Registration application via TaRMS portal under Tax Types tab.
  2. Upload sales invoices/contracts demonstrating $25,000 turnover threshold.
  3. Schedule physical business site inspection with regional ZIMRA station officer.
  4. ZIMRA inspector verifies operational status and fiscalization readiness.
  5. Approval granted; VAT number assigned and fiscal device integration authorized.
- Processing Time: 5 - 10 working days.

4. TAX CLEARANCE CERTIFICATE (ITF263) ISSUANCE (SVC-011)
- Category: Tax
- Requirements: Up-to-date Tax Returns filed (Income Tax, PAYE, QPDs), Fiscalization Compliance (Interfaced fiscal devices), Zero outstanding tax debt or agreed payment plan.
- Official Fee: USD $0 (Free official service).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Log into TaRMS Self-Service Portal account.
  2. Navigate to 'Services' -> 'Tax Clearance (ITF263)'.
  3. System performs automated compliance check against filing history and fiscalization status.
  4. If fully compliant, system auto-generates downloadable ITF263 Certificate with QR code.
  5. If non-compliant, system lists blocking items for resolution.
- Processing Time: Instantaneous (automated) upon compliance.
