JUDICIARY, IPEC & REGISTRATION PROCEDURES

1. DECEASED ESTATE ADMINISTRATION & EDICT MEETING (SVC-061)
- Agency: Master of the High Court (Judicial Service Commission)
- Category: civil_registration
- Requirements: Death Certificate, Death Notice Form BD 6, Inventory of Deceased's Assets, Marriage Certificate, Family Tree & List of Next of Kin, Original Will (if testate).
- Official Fee: USD $30 Edict Meeting Notice Fee + Master's Estate Commission (1.5% of estate value).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Register death notice at Master of High Court / Magistrates' Court office within 14 days.
  2. Master convenes Edict Meeting with surviving spouse and relatives to appoint Executor.
  3. Master issues Letters of Administration (Form MH 1) to appointed Executor.
  4. Executor publishes Liquidation and Distribution Account in Gazette.
  5. Estate distribution to beneficiaries upon account sign-off.
- Processing Time: 30 - 90 calendar days.

2. INSURANCE BROKER OPERATING LICENCE (SVC-072)
- Agency: Insurance and Pensions Commission (IPEC)
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Minimum Capital Proof ($100,000 USD), Professional Indemnity Insurance Cover ($200,000 USD), Qualified Principal Officer (Fellow/Associate of Insurance Institute), Company Incorporation Docs.
- Official Fee: USD $500 Application Fee + USD $2,000 Annual Operating Licence.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Submit licence application to IPEC Insurance Supervision Division.
  2. IPEC audits principal officer qualifications and professional indemnity cover.
  3. Commission evaluates financial solvency and capital reserves.
  4. Receive IPEC Insurance Broker Operating Licence.
- Processing Time: 30 - 60 calendar days.

3. SECURITIES INTERMEDIARY & STOCKBROKER LICENCE (SVC-098)
- Agency: Securities and Exchange Commission of Zimbabwe (SECZIM)
- Category: business_registration
- Requirements: Minimum Net Capital ($150,000 USD), Passed SECZIM Stockbroker Exams, CID Police Clearance, Financial Guarantee Fund Contribution, Form SEC 1.
- Official Fee: USD $1,000 Application Fee + USD $3,500 Annual Licence.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Pass SECZIM professional securities examination.
  2. Submit Form SEC 1 with company incorporation and capital proof.
  3. SECZIM Board conducts background vetting and financial audit.
  4. Receive Licensed Securities Intermediary / Stockbroker Certificate.
- Processing Time: 45 - 60 calendar days.
