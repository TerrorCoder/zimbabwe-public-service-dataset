CIVIL REGISTRY DEPARTMENT - ADMINISTRATIVE PROCEDURES & REQUIREMENTS

1. ORDINARY E-PASSPORT APPLICATION (SVC-001)
- Category: Civil Registration
- Requirements: Original National Identity Card (Metal, Plastic or Electronic), Original Birth Certificate (Long/Short Form), Marriage Certificate (if married female changing surname), Proof of Payment.
- Official Fee: USD $100 (Ordinary 7-14 working days turnaround). USD $20 application fee token.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Book appointment online at civilregistry.gov.zw or present at Civil Registry Office (Makombe Complex, Harare or Provincial Registry).
  2. Purchase application form and pay prescribed fee at cash office.
  3. Verification of original documents against National Population Register.
  4. Capture of biometric data (digital photograph, fingerprints, signature).
  5. Collection of e-passport upon presentation of collection slip and original ID.
- Processing Time: 7 - 14 working days.

2. EMERGENCY 24-HOUR E-PASSPORT APPLICATION (SVC-002)
- Category: Civil Registration
- Requirements: Original National ID Card, Original Birth Certificate, Proof of Emergency (e.g. medical letter, travel itinerary, official assignment letter), Proof of Payment.
- Official Fee: USD $200 (24-hour turnaround).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Present at Central Registry Passport Office.
  2. Request emergency processing endorsement at Supervisor's desk.
  3. Pay USD $200 fee at cash office.
  4. Priority biometric capture.
  5. Collect e-passport next working day.
- Processing Time: 24 - 48 hours.

3. BIRTH CERTIFICATE REGISTRATION (NEWBORN) (SVC-003)
- Category: Civil Registration
- Requirements: Hospital/Clinic Birth Confirmation Notification, Parents' Original National IDs, Parents' Marriage Certificate (if married), Witness ID (if home birth).
- Official Fee: USD $0 (Free within 42 days of birth). USD $2 for duplicate search.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Obtain yellow birth confirmation record from health facility where birth occurred.
  2. Both parents present at local Civil Registry office with IDs and birth record.
  3. Interview and verification of parents' status.
  4. Generation and issuance of long-form Birth Certificate.
- Processing Time: Same day / 1-2 hours.

4. NATIONAL IDENTITY CARD ISSUANCE & REPLACEMENT (SVC-004)
- Category: Civil Registration
- Requirements: Original Birth Certificate, Proof of residence (utility bill or chief's letter), Police report (if replacing lost ID).
- Official Fee: USD $0 for initial plastic/metal ID; USD $2-$5 for lost ID replacement.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Present at District Civil Registry sub-office.
  2. Verification of birth record against register.
  3. Facial photo capture and thumbprint impression.
  4. Printing of temporary green receipt / plastic ID card.
- Processing Time: Same day (temporary) / 14 days (metal/plastic card).
