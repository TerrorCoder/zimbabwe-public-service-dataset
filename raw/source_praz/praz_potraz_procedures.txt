PUBLIC PROCUREMENT REGULATORY AUTHORITY OF ZIMBABWE (PRAZ) & MINISTRY OF ICT / POTRAZ PROCEDURES

1. PRAZ SUPPLIER & BIDDER REGISTRATION / RENEWAL (SVC-012)
- Agency: Public Procurement Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (PRAZ)
- Category: Procurement
- Requirements: Certificate of Incorporation / PBC Certificate, Valid Tax Clearance Certificate (ITF263), CR6/CR5 or Member Details, Director National IDs, Company Profile, Category Selection.
- Official Fee: USD $120 per category per annum for local suppliers; USD $850 for international suppliers.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Access PRAZ Government Portal (portal.praz.org.zw).
  2. Create Supplier Account and complete profile.
  3. Select procurement categories (e.g., Stationery, ICT Hardware, Construction, Consulting).
  4. Upload valid Tax Clearance Certificate and Incorporation documents.
  5. Pay prescribed annual registration fee online or via bank transfer.
  6. Verification by PRAZ officer and issuance of PRAZ Supplier Certificate.
- Processing Time: 1 - 2 working days.

2. POSTAL AND COURIER SERVICE OPERATING LICENSE (SVC-013)
- Agency: Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ)
- Category: Postal Licensing
- Requirements: Registered Company Documents, Detailed Business Plan, Proof of Financial Capability, Security clearance for key personnel, Vehicle fleet/logistics list, Premises inspection.
- Official Fee: USD $500 Application Fee + USD $2,500 License Fee (Commercial Courier).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Collect application form from POTRAZ headquarters or website (potraz.gov.zw).
  2. Pay non-refundable application fee.
  3. Submit completed form with business plan, operational network map, and security clearance forms.
  4. POTRAZ Technical Committee conducts evaluation and physical site inspection.
  5. Board review and recommendation.
  6. Payment of initial license fee and issuance of Commercial Courier Operating License.
- Processing Time: 30 - 60 calendar days.

3. VALUE ADDED SERVICE (VAS) / INTERNET SERVICE PROVIDER (ISP) LICENSE (SVC-014)
- Agency: Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ)
- Category: Postal Licensing
- Requirements: Company Incorporation, Network Architecture & Topology Plan, Quality of Service Commitment, Data Protection Compliance Officer declaration, Application Fee.
- Official Fee: USD $1,000 Application Fee + USD $10,000 License Fee.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Submit Class License application to POTRAZ.
  2. Technical evaluation of proposed bandwidth and service architecture.
  3. Public notice and gazetting window.
  4. Payment of class license fee upon approval.
  5. Issuance of ISP/VAS Operating License.
- Processing Time: 45 - 90 calendar days.
