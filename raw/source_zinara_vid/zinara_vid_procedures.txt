ZINARA & VEHICLE INSPECTION DEPARTMENT (VID) - PROCEDURES

1. VEHICLE LICENCE DISC RENEWAL (ZINARA) (SVC-019)
- Agency: Zimbabwe National Roads Authority (ZINARA)
- Category: transport_licensing
- Requirements: Vehicle Registration Book (Logbook), Valid Third Party Vehicle Insurance Certificate, Radio Licence (ZBC token), Previous Licence Disc details.
- Official Fee: USD $20 per term (4 months) for standard light passenger vehicle.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Ensure valid third-party insurance cover is active.
  2. Visit ZINARA licensing office, selected ZimPost branch, or access online portal/bank app.
  3. Enter vehicle registration number and insurance policy details.
  4. Pay prescribed ZINARA road levy ($20) and ZBC radio licence fee.
  5. Collect or print valid Vehicle Licence Disc for display on windscreen.
- Processing Time: Immediate at counter / instant online.

2. PROVISIONAL DRIVER'S LICENCE APPLICATION (VID) (SVC-020)
- Agency: Vehicle Inspection Department (VID)
- Category: transport_licensing
- Requirements: Original National Identity Card / Valid Passport, 2 Passport-sized photos (white background), Eye Test Certificate / Verification, Age requirement (16 years for Class 4, 18 for Class 2).
- Official Fee: USD $20 examination fee.
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Report to nearest VID depot (e.g. VID Eastlea, Harare or VID Belvedere).
  2. Present National ID and pay $20 examination fee at cash counter.
  3. Undergo visual acuity eye test.
  4. Sit for 25-question Highway Code multiple choice written examination.
  5. Score minimum 88% (22/25) to pass.
  6. Receive laminated Provisional Driver's Licence valid for 12 months.
- Processing Time: Same day (2 - 3 hours).

3. CERTIFICATE OF FITNESS (COF) FOR COMMERCIAL VEHICLES (VID) (SVC-021)
- Agency: Vehicle Inspection Department (VID)
- Category: transport_licensing
- Requirements: Vehicle Logbook, Commercial Operator Permit, Fire Extinguisher, Red Warning Triangles, Emergency Medical Kit, Reflector Tapes.
- Official Fee: USD $30 - $50 depending on vehicle class (Taxis, Commuter Omnibuses, Heavy Goods Trucks).
- Procedure Steps:
  1. Drive commercial vehicle to VID inspection pit lane.
  2. Present registration book and pay inspection fee.
  3. VID examiner tests braking efficiency, steering mechanisms, tires, chassis, lights, and safety accessories.
  4. Upon passing mechanical audit, examiner stamps and issues Certificate of Fitness (COF) disc.
- Processing Time: Same day (1 - 2 hours).
